Preview

Your running plan, built from your Apple Health data and adjusted every week by a coach that runs on your iPhone. Pay once. No account, no subscription.

OpenStride reads your runs and heart rate from Apple Health and plans your training week around them. Each week the coach checks four things: • Load ratio: did this week jump far above your usual training? • Heart rate drift: are your easy runs too fast? • Time off: are you coming back from a break? • Last week: how much of the plan did you finish? If something is off, it changes the plan and tells you why in a short note. If everything is in range, the plan builds as scheduled. The Insights tab shows every number the coach used and every change it made. The coach is Gemma 3, an open-weight model that runs on your iPhone. With the on-device coach, your health data stays on your phone. No account or analytics. OpenStride is a training tool and does not give medical advice.

App Privacy

Data Not Collected

On-device coach

Plans are written by Gemma 3 on your iPhone. The model downloads once (2.6 GB, or 0.8 GB for the smaller one), then planning works offline.
